I am broadly interested in modeling users, content, and their interactions at scale, and in advancing the state-of-the-art across the machine learning technologies underpinning this, including graph and sequential representation learning, generative recommendation, and large language and foundation models for content and user understanding, as well as applications to user personalization and safety problems. At Pinterest, I lead teams across horizontal applied research and engineering efforts spanning user understanding, content understanding, and exploration. Previously, I led user modeling and personalization research at Snap, where my team's work led to multiple step-function research platform capabilities and 105+ launches with topline business impact across Growth, Content, Ads, Lenses, and Safety ML surfaces.

Before industry, I got my PhD in the [Computer Science Department](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/) at [Carnegie Mellon University](http://www.cmu.edu/index.shtml), where I worked on modeling and discovery of various abuse vectors in large online platforms. I was fortunate to have been advised by [Christos Faloutsos](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/~christos/). Earlier, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from the [Department of Computer Science](http://www.csc.ncsu.edu/) at [North Carolina State University](http://www.ncsu.edu/). There, I worked with [Nagiza Samatova](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vukA8JAAAAAJ&hl=en) on reduction, indexing, and storage systems for large-scale scientific data.
